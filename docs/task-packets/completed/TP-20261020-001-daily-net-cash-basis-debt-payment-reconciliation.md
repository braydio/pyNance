## Verified cause and user intent
The user reports Daily Net materially understating *money paid out*. Current calculation is not actual cash out: `backend/app/services/transaction_flow_classification.py::rollup_by_day` intentionally keeps `debt_settlement`, `investment_movement`, `unclassified_outflows` OUTSIDE `expenses` and `net`; `backend/app/routes/charts.py::get_daily_net` exposes only economic fields as the main series. That explains some but not necessarily all gaps. `classify_reporting_flow` classifies `primary=LOAN_PAYMENTS` as debt settlement; `TRANSFER_OUT` without verified pair as unclassified; positive `is_internal` flag is accepted as a transfer even if pair missing. `load_classified_flows` excludes hidden/pending/out-of-scope accounts. These are confirmed code observations, **not verified amounts for the user's bank ledger**.

User wants debt payments and every actual external payment visible in a chart of outgo, rather than treating only purchase-time economic spending as their expenses. The exact exception for *external cash movement* must be VERIFIED transfers BETWEEN USER-OWNED ACCOUNTS, not all P2P transactions: payment to another person is normally a real outflow and cannot simply disappear. Keep unknown movements visible and reviewable.

## Product/financial contract: two labelled measures, no double count
- **Default Dashboard presentation: Money In / Money Out / Net Cash Movement** from posted *funding/cash-account* legs, per inclusive selected range. Money Out INCLUDES checking/debit/savings cash paid for card/loan installments, direct consumer payments, investment contributions (separate `Allocated to investments` subcategory), P2P sent to other people, and unknown debits (subgroup `Needs classification`).
- **Secondary Economic Activity view:** purchase-time expense on credit card or checking, interest fees, refunds, income earned. Later principal/card payment is not an additional economic expense. It retains current `income,expenses,refunds,net` API shapes as historical compatibility.
- Treat liability-account purchases/payments and linked account receiving legs as non-funding cash movements so a $500 purchase plus later $500 payment does **not** show $1,000 Money Out. A card payment funded from checking counts $500 once in the cash mode, and the card purchase $500 once in economic mode, never together as one “expense” total. Loans: total payment is a cash outflow, sourced interest is economic expense once, principal decreases liability. Loan proceeds are financing inflow, NOT earned wages, even if they increase bank cash. Investment transfers reduce bank cash while growing assets; include in Money Out but distinguish from consumption.
- Distinguish `Money Out` vs `Expenses` conspicuously: do not conflate financial profit with liquidity. If backend cannot identify funding legs reliably, show `Incomplete coverage` and excluded amounts, not a fabricated 0.
